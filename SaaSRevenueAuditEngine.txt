Business Scenario

You are working as a Data Analyst for a B2B SaaS company.

Management reports that:

Subscription revenue is growing
Customer cancellations are also increasing
Finance reports higher revenue than the Customer Success team
Some high-paying customers have very low product usage
Support escalations are increasing
Renewal performance differs significantly by region and plan

The CEO wants to know:

"Are we actually growing sustainably, or are we increasing revenue while creating future churn and revenue leakage?"

Build an analytical solution using Python, SQL and Power BI.

Part 1: Python

Create an object-oriented Python solution named SaaSRevenueAuditEngine.

Your solution must load all four sheets and create a reusable data validation and transformation pipeline.

Validate and handle at minimum:

Duplicate or missing subscription, invoice, usage and case IDs.
Missing customer IDs.
Subscription renewal dates earlier than signup dates.
Negative or missing monthly fees.
Discount percentages outside 0 to 100.
Active subscriptions with logically inconsistent renewal information.
Invoices linked to subscriptions that do not exist.
Negative invoice or tax amounts.
Paid invoices without valid payment dates.
Payment dates before invoice dates.
Negative login counts or active minutes.
Usage records linked to customers not present in subscription data.
Support cases closed before they were opened.
Negative resolution hours.
CSAT scores outside 1 to 5.
Closed support cases with missing closure information.

Do not simply delete every incorrect record.

Classify each record as:

VALID
SUSPICIOUS
INVALID

Create a column explaining the reason for the classification.

Using the cleaned data, create these customer-level metrics:

Healthy
